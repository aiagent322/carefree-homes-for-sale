Carefree Golf Course & Resort Community Homes

Carefree and its immediate surroundings offer three distinct golf communities, each with a different ownership model, course pedigree, and residential product. Understanding the differences matters before committing to a specific property.

The Boulders — Jay Morrish Courses

Two 18-hole Jay Morrish championship courses — Boulders North and Boulders South — weave through the 1,300-acre resort and residential community. The arrangement is unique: courses alternate daily between resort-guest access and member access, so residential members always have a course available. The club is a combination of resort and private membership structure. Membership availability, initiation fees, and annual assessments fluctuate; confirm current terms directly with the club before purchasing for golf access. Homes adjacent to the fairways range from $1.5M for patio-style properties to $5M+ for estate homes on premium view lots.

Desert Forest Golf Club — Tom Fazio Design

A historic private 18-hole course in Carefree's far north, anchoring the Carefree Ranch Highlands corridor. Desert Forest is widely regarded as one of the most significant golf course designs in Arizona — known for its naturalistic routing through the Sonoran Desert with minimal grading, preserving the landscape rather than imposing on it. Private club; confirm membership availability and terms directly with the club. Homes in the surrounding estate communities range $2M–$6M+ on lots of 1 to 3 acres.

Rancho Manana Golf Club — Semi-Private

Located just over the Carefree–Cave Creek border on Cave Creek Road. A semi-private par-70 course with dramatic elevation changes and panoramic valley views, consistently ranked among Arizona's top courses and among the top 50 nationally for women golfers. Daily tee times available to the public; semi-private membership provides preferred access. Homes in the Rancho Manana neighborhood start around $700K and run to $1.5M+ for premium course-view lots.

What to Verify Before Buying for Golf

Club membership at all three is separate from the home purchase unless otherwise noted. Membership availability is not guaranteed simply because you purchase an adjacent property. At The Boulders specifically, some casitas are in the resort rental pool — rental pool terms may restrict the owner's own golf access windows. Confirm membership terms, availability, and any residency requirements with each club before going under contract.
