Carefree Property Types

Carefree's residential market spans a wider range than the luxury median suggests. Here is the full product breakdown across the 85377 zip code.

  • Condos & Resort Casitas — Carefree Casas from $245K; Boulders casitas to $1.2M+, some in the resort rental pool.
  • Custom & Luxury Estates — The dominant Carefree product. Desert contemporary and Southwestern homes on 0.5–3 acres, $1.1M–$7M+. No local real estate tax.
  • Golf & Resort Community Homes — The Boulders (Jay Morrish), Desert Forest (Tom Fazio), Rancho Manana (semi-private). $1.5M–$6M+.
  • SkyRanch Airpark Townhomes — Patio townhomes in Arizona's first fly-in community. $700K–$1.1M. T-hangar access varies by unit.
