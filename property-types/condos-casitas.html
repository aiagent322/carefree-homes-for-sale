Carefree Condos & Casitas For Sale

Carefree's attached housing market is small — roughly 15 to 25 active listings across five distinct communities at any given time — but it spans a wider price range than most buyers expect. Entry pricing starts near $245K at Carefree Casas and climbs to $1.2M+ for premium Boulders casitas with resort rental income potential.

Carefree Casas — From $245K

Located on Tranquil Trail in the 85377 zip code. Small-footprint 1-bedroom units around 800 square feet. A December 2025 comparable closed at $245K (37618 N Tranquil Trail #9). Walking distance to the Carefree Town Center and Sundial Circle. HOA dues cover shared maintenance; request the current reserve study before writing.

Carefree Terrace Condominiums — $350K–$900K

On Tom Darlington Drive just east of the Carefree Town Center. A mix of townhome and condo configurations with walkable access to Easy Street restaurants, galleries, and the weekly Farmers Market. Size and renovation level drive most of the price spread within this complex.

Carefree Town Center Condos — $350K–$900K

Mixed-use attached units near Easy Street and Ho Drive — the most walkable residential product in all of Carefree. True pedestrian access to the Sundial Circle plaza, boutique retail, and the Carefree Desert Gardens botanical park.

Boulders Casitas — $825K–$1.2M+

Approximately 550 sq ft southwestern-style attached units inside the 1,300-acre Boulders Resort and residential community. The defining variable: some casitas are grandfathered into The Boulders Resort rental pool, allowing owners to earn rental revenue while not in residence. This is a meaningful feature for second-home buyers — but the rental pool terms, owner-use windows, and HOA rules vary by specific unit. Confirm participation status, current terms, and withdrawal options during due diligence. The Boulders straddles Carefree (85377) and Scottsdale (85266); both fall within Cave Creek Unified School District.

What to Check on Any Carefree Condo

Carefree's condo market is thin enough that HOA reserve fund health is a material variable. A small complex with deferred maintenance and inadequate reserves can issue large special assessments on short notice. Request the most recent reserve study and the last 12 months of HOA board minutes before writing on any attached product. For Boulders casitas, confirm rental pool participation status, current occupancy agreements, and whether the specific unit can be withdrawn from the pool if your plans change.
