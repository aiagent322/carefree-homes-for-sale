Carefree Homes For Sale Under $1 Million

The Carefree market is predominantly above $1M, but an under-$1M segment exists across three distinct product types. These are not compromises — they are different products with different trade-offs that suit a specific kind of buyer.

Carefree Casas Condos — From $245K

The entry point for ownership in the 85377 zip code. One-bedroom condos on Tranquil Trail, approximately 800 sq ft. A December 2025 comp closed at $245K. Walking distance to the Carefree Town Center and Sundial Circle. Cave Creek Unified School District. HOA dues cover maintenance; verify reserve fund health before writing.

Carefree Terrace & Town Center Condos — $350K–$900K

Attached townhome and condo product near the Town Center — the most walkable residential option in Carefree. Easy Street restaurants, the Farmers Market, galleries, and the Carefree Desert Gardens all within walking distance. Sizes and renovation quality drive the price spread within this range.

SkyRanch Townhomes — $700K–$1.1M

Patio townhomes in Arizona's first fly-in community. Shared pool, tennis, and pickleball. Some units come with T-hangar access; others require a separate arrangement. Units at the lower end of the SkyRanch range (pre-renovation, no hangar) can approach the under-$1M threshold. Confirm hangar status and HOA reserve health before writing.

Original Townsite Homes — Original Condition

A small number of original-condition 1960s–70s townsite homes trade below $900K when they need full renovation. The lot value and location are the assets; the structure is the variable. Buyers considering these should budget renovation costs into the total acquisition price before writing.
