Carefree Condos & Attached Homes

Carefree has a small but real attached-housing market — condos, casitas, and patio homes spread across five identifiable communities. With roughly 15–25 active listings in this category at any given time, this is a thin inventory segment where patience and direct agent relationships matter more than in the broader market.

Carefree Casas

Located on Tranquil Trail in the 85377 zip code. Small-footprint condos — 1-bedroom units around 800 square feet — at Cave Creek's most affordable price point. Entry pricing in the $240K–$300K range. A December 2025 comparable: 37618 N Tranquil Trail #9 closed at $245K. Walking distance to the Carefree Town Center and Sundial Circle. HOA dues cover shared maintenance; confirm current dues and reserves before writing.

Carefree Terrace Condominiums

Located on Tom Darlington Drive, just east of the Carefree Town Center. A townhome and condo enclave with a town-center lifestyle — walkable to Easy Street restaurants, galleries, and the Farmers Market. Pricing ranges $350K–$900K depending on size, level, and renovation quality.

Boulders Casitas

The upper tier of Carefree's condo-scale product — 550 sq ft southwestern-style attached units within The Boulders Resort community. Some are grandfathered into The Boulders Resort rental pool with revenue-earning potential. Entry pricing around $825K; premium units with course or boulder views can exceed $1.2M. The resort rental pool terms vary by unit; confirm specific participation status during due diligence.

Carefree Town Center Condos

Mixed-use condos in the Easy Street and Ho Drive area — walking distance to the Sundial and Town Center retail. Lower-density attached housing with a true walkable town-center lifestyle. Pricing varies $350K–$900K.

What to Check

Carefree's condo market is thin enough that HOA reserve fund health is a material variable — a small complex with deferred maintenance and inadequate reserves can assess large special fees on short notice. Request the most recent reserve study and the last 12 months of HOA meeting minutes before writing on any Carefree attached product. For Boulders casitas specifically, confirm rental pool participation terms and current occupancy agreements.
