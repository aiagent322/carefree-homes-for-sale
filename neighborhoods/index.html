Carefree Neighborhood Guide

Carefree spans 8.8 square miles with fewer than 3,800 residents — a small market where neighborhood selection determines your daily life more than in larger communities. This directory covers every residential area in the 85377 zip code, from the resort casitas of The Boulders to Arizona's original fly-in airpark at SkyRanch.

Established Communities

  • The Boulders — 1,300-acre resort and golf community straddling Carefree and Scottsdale. Casitas $825K+, custom homes $1.5M–$5M+. Two Jay Morrish courses. Resort spa and amenities.
  • Downtown & Original Townsite — Original 1960s–70s master-planned core, half a mile from the Sundial Circle. 0.3–1 acre lots, $700K–$1.5M. True walkability to Easy Street restaurants, galleries, and the Farmers Market.
  • Carefree Ranch & Highlands — Estate tier in Carefree's far north bordering Tonto National Forest. Desert Forest Golf Club (Tom Fazio). Custom homes on 1–3 acres, $2M–$6M+.

Unique Communities

  • SkyRanch at Carefree — Arizona's first fly-in residential community, operating since the 1950s. Patio townhomes $700K–$1.1M. Some units include T-hangar access.
  • Condos & Casitas — Carefree Casas (Tranquil Trail, from $245K), Carefree Terrace Condominiums, Carefree Town Center condos, and Boulders casitas. The full attached-housing inventory in 85377.
